=== SBI BANK - LOAN POLICIES & ELIGIBILITY ===

--- HOME LOAN ---
Eligibility: Salaried employees (min ₹25,000/month), Self-employed (min 3 years business), age 18–70 years.
Max Tenure: 30 years
Processing Fee: 0.35% of loan amount (min ₹2,000, max ₹10,000) + GST
Prepayment Charges: NIL for floating rate loans

--- PERSONAL LOAN ---
Eligibility: Salaried with min ₹15,000 net monthly income, age 21–58 years, min 2 years work experience.
Amount: ₹25,000 to ₹20 lakh
Tenure: 6 months to 6 years
Processing Fee: 1.50% + GST

--- CAR LOAN ---
Eligibility: Salaried (min ₹2.5 lakh p.a.) or self-employed (min ₹4 lakh p.a.), age 21–67 years.
Margin: 10–15% of vehicle cost
Tenure: Up to 7 years
Processing Fee: ₹1,000–₹1,500 + GST

--- EDUCATION LOAN ---
Courses Covered: Graduation, Post-graduation, Professional courses in India & abroad
Collateral: Required above ₹7.5 lakh
Moratorium: Course period + 1 year
Tax Benefit: Interest deductible under Section 80E

--- GOLD LOAN ---
LTV Ratio: Up to 75% of gold value
Interest Rate: 8.75% p.a. onwards
Tenure: Up to 3 years
Processing Fee: 0.50% + GST
Minimum Loan: ₹20,000

=== SBI BANK - ACCOUNT OPENING POLICY ===

Savings Account Types:
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD): Zero balance, for all citizens
- Regular Savings Account: Min ₹1,000 balance
- SBI Salary Account: Zero balance for employees
- SBI Senior Citizen Account: Extra FD rates + benefits
- SBI Minor Account (Pehla Kadam): For children, joint with parent

Documents Required (KYC):
- Identity Proof: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License
- Address Proof: Aadhaar, utility bills (not older than 3 months), rental agreement
- Photographs: 2 passport-size photos
- For minors: Birth certificate + guardian ID proof

=== INTEREST RATE POLICY ===

Benchmark Rate: RLLR (Repo Linked Lending Rate) = Repo Rate + Spread
Current Repo Rate: 6.50% (subject to RBI revision)
MCLR (1-year): 8.85%

Savings Account Interest: 2.70% p.a. up to ₹1 crore; 3.00% above ₹1 crore

=== INSURANCE PRODUCTS ===

SBI Life Insurance: Term plans, ULIPs, endowment plans — available via SBI branches
SBI General Insurance: Home, health, motor, travel insurance
SBI Annuity Deposit: Guaranteed monthly income scheme for retired individuals

Pradhan Mantri Schemes via SBI:
- PMJJBY (Jeevan Jyoti): ₹2 lakh life cover at ₹436/year
- PMSBY (Suraksha Bima): ₹2 lakh accident cover at ₹20/year
- APY (Atal Pension Yojana): Guaranteed pension ₹1,000–₹5,000/month
